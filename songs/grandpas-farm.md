### Lyrics

```text
[Spoken Intro]
Good morning, friends!
Today we're going to Grandpa's farm!
Listen closely... and shout out who's making the sound!
Ready? Let's go!

[Chorus]
Grandpa, Grandpa, your farm is so sweet,
So many animal friends to meet!
We hear the sound, and we say the name,
Grandpa, Grandpa, let's play the game!

[Verse 1 - Rooster]
Cock-a-doodle-doo! Who says cock-a-doodle-doo?
It's the rooster, waking Grandpa, waking you!
Early, early, up with the sun,
Cock-a-doodle-doo! The day's begun!
Who's next, Grandpa? Who's next?

[Verse 2 - Cow]
Moo, moo! Who says moo?
It's the cow! She's Grandpa's friend, and yours too!
She gives us milk, so white and yummy,
Glug, glug, glug, it's in my tummy!

[Chorus]
Grandpa, Grandpa, your farm is so sweet,
So many animal friends to meet!
We hear the sound, and we say the name,
Grandpa, Grandpa, let's play the game!

[Verse 3 - Hen]
Cluck, cluck! Who says cluck?
It's the hen! Fresh eggs for breakfast, what luck!
Her fluffy chicks don't want to sleep,
They run around: cheep, cheep, cheep!
Who's next, Grandpa? Who's next?

[Verse 4 - Sheep]
Baa, baa! Who says baa?
It's the sheep! So soft and fluffy, ooh, aah!
Soft wool makes a hat and a scarf, ta-da!
Now we're warm and cozy! Baa, baa, baa!

[Chorus]
Grandpa, Grandpa, your farm is so sweet,
So many animal friends to meet!
We hear the sound, and we say the name,
Grandpa, Grandpa, let's play the game!

[Verse 5 - Duck]
Quack, quack! Who says quack?
It's the duck! Splish, splash, swimming there and back!
Waddle, waddle, wiggle, wiggle,
Let's all waddle! Quack, quack, quack!

[Outro]
Moo! Cluck! Baa! Quack! Cock-a-doodle-doo!
We're all friends on Grandpa's farm, me and you!
Bye-bye, rooster! Bye-bye, cow!
Bye-bye, sheep and duck and hen! We're going now!
So many friends we got to meet,
Grandpa, Grandpa, your farm is so sweet!

[Spoken Outro]
Bye-bye, friends! See you in the next song!

[End]
```
