# TikTok France monetization plan (research, Oct 2026)

Researched with 4 parallel researchers + 4 fact-checkers. Every number is from third-party or TikTok sources linked below; items under "Unverified" must be checked in the TikTok app.

## Monetization ladder (in unlock order)

### 1. TikTok Shop affiliate (you earn a commission when people buy a product you show)
- **Unlock:** You must be 18+. The follower minimum is disputed: 500 (a French guide citing a policy dated 3 Sept 2026, https://www.formalive.fr/affiliation-tiktok-shop/), 1,000, or 1,500 (https://www.shopify.com/fr/blog/affiliation-tiktok). Sign-up reportedly asks for four things: an EU passport or ID card or 'other valid EU-issued ID', proof of a French address, bank details (RIB), and a statement that you work as an 'entrepreneur individuel' (https://kohenavocats.com/affiliation-tiktok-shop-siret-mention-publicite-commission-retenue-recours-2026/). French guides say you need a 'professional' account, but a fact-checker doubts this. Check in the app before switching, because Creator Rewards requires a Personal account.
- **Earnings:** Each seller sets its own commission. Shopify France reports 5–20% per sale (https://www.shopify.com/fr/blog/affiliation-tiktok); other guides say 15–25% (unverified). Example only, not real data: 20 sales a month of a €25 product at 15% = €75 a month. A low-confidence US-only statistic puts the median at about $95 a month for affiliates with 1k–10k followers (https://www.dashboardly.io/statistics/tiktok-shop-affiliate-creator-statistics). Commission is reportedly paid about 15 days after delivery and cancelled if the buyer returns the item within 30 days (https://speed-ecom.eu/blog/tiktok-shop-france-2026-guide-complet-vendre/, unverified). Ignore promises of '€3–5k a month'; they are marketing.
- **France:** Available. TikTok Shop opened in France on 31 March 2025, and France became its largest European market (https://www.strategies.fr/actualites/culture-tech/LQ4420933C/tiktok-shop-arrive-en-france-le-31-mars.html, https://www.ecommerce-nation.fr/six-mois-apres-son-lancement-en-france-tiktok-shop-seduit-deja-plus-de-16-000-marchands/).

### 2. LIVE Gifts (viewers send you virtual gifts during a LIVE)
- **Unlock:** You must be 18+ to go LIVE and to receive gifts (TikTok Community Guidelines, in force since 24 Sep 2026: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/OpenTermsArchive/pga-versions/main/TikTok/Community%20Guidelines.md). The usual threshold is 1,000 followers (French 2026 guides, e.g. https://www.livechat-ai.fr/blog/vendre-sur-tiktok-live-france-guide-2026). Lower thresholds of 500–800 are reportedly rolling out in parts of Europe (https://tiktokstats.com/guides/tiktok-live-content-monetization-requirements-2026-guide, low confidence). Some French blogs still say 16+; that rule ended in November 2022.
- **Earnings:** How it works: fans buy Coins, send Gifts, and you receive Diamonds. The common unofficial rate is 1 Diamond ≈ US$0.005, and you get Diamonds worth about 50% of the gift's Coin value. Measured against what the fan actually paid, that is only about 15–25% (https://www.youdeo.fr/valeur-diamant-tiktok/, https://diamonds.ttmoneycalculator.com/). Worked example using those rates: a fan who spends €10 on Coins in the app gives you about €1.20–1.30. The withdrawal minimum is reportedly about US$100, so fans need to spend roughly €600–900 before your first cash-out (unverified). With 'Scaled LIVE Rewards', SELECT creators can get up to 53%: 40% from per-LIVE missions plus 13% from weekly missions (https://www.tiktok.com/creator-academy/article/scaled-live-rewards). Third-party estimate for creators with 1K–10K followers who go LIVE regularly: about US$50–300 a month (https://tiktokstats.com/articles/how-much-do-tiktok-live-streamers-earn-complete-guide, unverified). Realistically, expect €0 before 1,000 followers, then often only tens of euros a month for many months. Meaningful LIVE income usually takes months of near-daily LIVEs of 1+ hour.
- **France:** Available in France. Check the exact follower threshold in your own app.

### 3. Direct brand deals and free products (brands pay you, or send products, to feature them)
- **Unlock:** Direct deals have no official minimum: brands contact small creators, or you pitch them yourself. Below 10K followers, most offers are free products, which still count as taxable income. TikTok One (the former Creator Marketplace) reportedly needs about 10,000 followers, age 18+ and 3+ posts in the last 28–30 days; the France threshold is unverified (https://www.stackmatix.com/blog/tiktok-creator-marketplace-guide).
- **Earnings:** Agency and blog estimates for France: about €50–250 per video at 1K–10K followers (https://businessbacon.fr/prix-influenceur/). At around 10K followers: about €250–600 for a simple post and €400–900 for a product integration (https://nthg.fr/estimateur-tarif-tiktok/, https://www.shopify.com/fr/blog/prix-influenceur). An agency study of 600+ French deals found a median of €3,000 per collaboration for creators with 10K–50K followers (2025 to mid-2026). Those deals were agency-negotiated and multi-platform, so treat €3,000 as a ceiling, not a beginner rate (https://mtrchk.com/fr/etude-prix-influence-2026). A written contract is mandatory once deals with one partner reach €1,000 excl. VAT in a year.
- **France:** Allowed, but under the French influencer law: ad labels, written contracts and banned products (see legal section).

### 4. LIVE Subscription (fans pay you monthly for perks during your LIVEs)
- **Unlock:** Commonly cited: age 18+, at least 1,000 followers, and at least 30 minutes of LIVE in the last 28 days (https://www.dexerto.com/tiktok/what-are-tiktok-live-subscriptions-and-how-do-they-work-2971357/). Sources conflict: some creators report needing 5K–10K followers, and other sources say 10K followers plus 100K monthly views.
- **Earnings:** No reliable figures for France. Outside the US and Canada, the reported share is 50% of net subscription revenue, plus a bonus of up to 20% (maximum 70%) for creators with 10,000+ followers and 1M+ video views in the last month (https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/tiktok-expands-creator-subscription-payouts-us-canada/801996/, October 2025, unverified).
- **France:** The feature exists; the exact thresholds for France are unclear.

### 5. Creator Rewards Program (TikTok pays you for views on videos longer than 1 minute)
- **Unlock:** Account requirements: age 18+, a PERSONAL account (Business accounts are excluded), at least 10,000 followers, at least 100,000 views in the last 30 days, good standing, real followers and views, no VPN, and an account registered in France (https://tiktrendsonline.com/tiktok-creator-rewards-program-france/, https://swello.com/fr/blog/gagner-de-largent-sur-tiktok/). Video requirements: at least 1 minute long (creators advise going over 1:01 to be safe), original, and at least 1,000 qualified views before the video earns anything. Duets, Stitches, photo posts and ads never earn (https://tokconnect.com/guides/tiktok-creator-rewards-program/). A qualified view is a unique For You view of 5+ seconds, or a Search view of 30+ seconds (search views count since a 'December 4' update, probably 2025). Views where the viewer tapped 'Not interested' do not count.
- **Earnings:** TikTok publishes no rate, so all figures are third-party. Reported pay for French views ranges from €0.25 to €2.20 per 1,000 qualified views, with €0.50–0.80 typical (https://linkdash.eu/en/blog/tiktok-creator-rewards-europe-2026-realistic-earnings-by-country-uk-13, https://www.elev8or.io/blog/tiktok-creator-rewards-rpm-2026, https://tiktrendsonline.com/tiktok-creator-rewards-program-france/). That makes 100K qualified views about €50–80, and 1M about €500–800 (anywhere from €250 to €2,200). Example: about 300K qualified views a month at $0.60 per 1,000 ≈ $180 a month (https://stackinfluence.com/blog/tiktok-pay-per-view-what-creators-earn-in-2026). One creator reported only about €60 for 800K views (https://www.influenth.com/combien-tiktok-paye-vue-2026-vrais-revenus-createurs-devoiles/). LinkDash says most European creators earn under £40 a month from it, and many creators reported sharp drops in early 2026 (https://eurweb.com/tiktok-payout-drop/, anecdotal). Payment comes on the 15th of the month; the program terms say a $50 minimum, while the Help Center says $10. You must complete tax info and a payment method.
- **France:** France is eligible. Several 2026 sources agree, and the Creator Academy list updated in August 2026 names the US, UK, Germany, Japan, South Korea, France, Mexico and Brazil (https://socialrails.com/blog/tiktok-creator-fund-eligible-countries). Egypt, the UAE and Saudi Arabia are NOT eligible.

### 6. Video Subscriptions (fans pay monthly for subscriber-only videos)
- **Unlock:** Age 18+, at least 10,000 followers and at least 100,000 video views in the last calendar month. There is also a separate path through LIVE eligibility. You must post your first subscriber-only video within the month, then at least 3 per month. You can offer up to 3 tiers at prices you choose (https://www.tiktok.com/creator-academy/fr/article/Subscription).
- **Earnings:** No reliable public figures for France. The reported revenue share is the same as for LIVE Subscription: 50% base, up to 70% with the bonus (unverified). It works best when you have extra content to sell, such as full lessons or full recipes.
- **France:** Available in France since September 2024 (https://iphonesoft.fr/2024/09/23/tiktok-annonce-abonnements-payants-createurs-disponible-france).

### 7. TikTok Series (paid collections of videos)
- **Unlock:** Age 18+, an account at least 30 days old, a public account, 10,000 followers (fewer if you can prove you sell premium content elsewhere), 3+ public posts, and 1,000+ views in the last 30 days (https://community.skeepers.io/fr/blog/tiktok-series/).
- **Earnings:** A collection holds up to 80 videos of up to 20 minutes each, priced at $0.99–$189.99. No earnings data was found for France.
- **France:** Unclear. French articles say it is available, but no official confirmation for France was found, so check in your app. A claimed easing of the requirements in January 2026 was not found in any source.

## Niche ideas

### Learn Arabic (and Egyptian Arabic) in short lessons explained in French
- **Face:** Faceless: your voice plus on-screen text or simple animation. Later, LIVE lessons with your voice only.
- **Language:** French narration with the Arabic words on screen (bilingual)
- **Why:** This is a skill few French creators have, and originality is the main filter for Creator Rewards (https://www.gemlist.io/blog/tiktok-creator-rewards-requirements). The viewers you want (French people, students, Arab families in France) are mostly in France, an eligible country, so their views can count. Education is one of the niches reported to pay above average; some sources report more than €2 per 1,000 qualified views for finance and education (https://tiktrendsonline.com/tiktok-creator-rewards-program-france/). Search views of 30+ seconds now count (https://tokconnect.com/guides/tiktok-creator-rewards-program/), and people search for lessons. One condition: your French must be good enough to explain clearly. You can write the script first and add French captions.
- **Feeds:** Creator Rewards (lessons of 1–2 minutes), video Subscriptions or Series (full lesson packs), LIVE gifts (live voice Q&A lessons), brand deals (learning products).

### Egyptian and Middle-Eastern home cooking, filmed hands-only
- **Face:** Faceless: your hands, the food and your voice
- **Language:** French voiceover with the Arabic dish names on screen. A separate Arabic version is possible.
- **Why:** Quick cooking is listed among the popular niches for Arabic-speaking audiences (low-confidence source: http://www.greecewiki.com/tik-tok-arabic-culture-arab-tiktok-culture/). Made in French, it reaches viewers in France. A full recipe naturally runs past 1 minute, which Creator Rewards requires. You film it all yourself, so it is original. On TikTok Shop France, Household Appliances is one of the top categories, at about €11.7M in sales (third-party data: https://blog.lengow.com/fr/beaute-et-soin-personnel-sur-tiktok-shop-france-le-premier-decryptage-du-marche/), which gives kitchen-tool affiliate options.
- **Feeds:** TikTok Shop affiliate (kitchen tools you have actually tested), Creator Rewards, brand deals (food and kitchen brands), LIVE cook-alongs with voice and hands.

### Craft, activity and screen-free ideas FOR PARENTS (reusing your kids-channel know-how)
- **Face:** Faceless: hands, materials and your voice. No children on camera.
- **Language:** French
- **Why:** It reuses what you already know from your kids YouTube channel but speaks to adults, and that matters on TikTok. You must be 13+ to have an account, the under-13 version exists only in the US, and EU users aged 13–17 get no personalised ads. A child audience is therefore small and worth little to advertisers (TikTok Community Guidelines 2026: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/OpenTermsArchive/pga-versions/main/TikTok/Community%20Guidelines.md; https://techcrunch.com/2023/08/04/tiktok-algorithm-dsa/). Parents are adults who can buy. Keeping your own children off camera keeps you clear of the French child-influencer law (loi 2020-1266). Do not reupload your YouTube kids videos as they are; make new, adult-facing videos.
- **Feeds:** Brand deals (family and craft brands), TikTok Shop affiliate (only safe products you tested, no cheap marketplace toys), and Creator Rewards if videos run past 1 minute.

### Life in France explained in Egyptian Arabic (daily life, prices, studying, work, your own experience)
- **Face:** Faceless or voice-only: your voice over footage you film yourself (streets, shops, blurred documents) or screen recordings. LIVE Q&A with voice only.
- **Language:** Egyptian Arabic
- **Why:** HONEST PICTURE: the audience is huge (Egypt alone has about 47.5M adults reachable by TikTok ads, https://affectgroup.com/blog/tiktok-ads-audience-in-egypt-2026-breakdown/, unverified), but Creator Rewards will probably pay you very little for it. Egypt, the Gulf and the Maghreb are not Creator Rewards countries. Secondary sources say only views from eligible countries count: the US, UK, Germany, France, Japan, South Korea, Brazil and Mexico (https://quasa.io/media/tiktok-creator-rewards-program-eligible-countries-in-2026, unverified; check in the app). It is not proven that TikTok sends Arabic videos mainly to viewers in the Middle East and North Africa; TikTok's own 2020 explainer says language and country settings weigh less than how viewers interact (https://newsroom.tiktok.com/en-us/how-tiktok-recommends-videos-for-you). Viewers outside France probably cannot buy from TikTok Shop France (inference). So in this niche the money comes mostly from LIVE gifts (gifts can come from anywhere) and brand deals, plus a little Creator Rewards from Arabic speakers living in France. One profile cites a creator earning $1,000–3,000 a month from 4 hours of LIVE a day, but that is one anecdote, not typical. Share your experience rather than legal or immigration advice, and avoid political topics: political accounts cannot monetize.
- **Feeds:** LIVE gifts (main), brand deals, a small amount of Creator Rewards (viewers in France only), later Subscriptions.

## First 90 days

### Days 1–7: set up correctly
1) Confirm you are 18+ (required for every money feature). 2) Check that your residence permit (titre de séjour) allows self-employed work, which you will need later for a micro-entreprise and TikTok Shop (https://www.indy.fr/blog/creer-micro-entreprise-etranger/). 3) Create the account on your phone in France, with your French SIM and NO VPN. The region is fixed at sign-up, and VPN use disqualifies you from Creator Rewards (https://tiktrendsonline.com/tiktok-creator-rewards-program-france/). 4) Keep it a Personal account; do not switch to Business. 5) Write a short French bio that makes clear an adult runs the account. 6) Pick ONE niche from the list. 7) Watch the most popular videos in that niche and note their first seconds, length, captions and on-screen text. 8) Write a list of video ideas and script your first videos. 9) Post your first 3 test videos.

**Milestone:** Account live with a France region and a Personal account type; niche chosen; first 3 videos posted.

### Weeks 2–4: test formats
Post at least 3–5 videos a week; growth guides link that pace to reaching 10K (https://www.wavevision.io/blog/how-to-grow-from-0-to-10k-followers-on-tiktok-a-data-driven-playbook-for-2026, marketing source). Make most videos just over 1 minute (1:01+) so your habit already fits Creator Rewards. Put a strong hook in the first seconds, because a For You view only counts after 5 seconds. Use searchable words in your caption, on-screen text and voice, since Search views of 30+ seconds now count (https://tokconnect.com/guides/tiktok-creator-rewards-program/). Try a few different formats in your niche. Reply to comments. Check analytics every week: watch time, finish rate, search value and engagement are the factors TikTok uses to calculate pay (https://newsroom.tiktok.com/introducing-the-new-creator-rewards-program). If you reuse any material from your YouTube channel, remove the YouTube logo and add something new.

**Milestone:** You know which 1–2 formats keep people watching longest.

### Weeks 5–8: double down and unlock first income
Make most videos in your best format and keep posting 3–5+ a week. When you pass 500–1,500 followers, check in the app whether TikTok Shop affiliate is open to you and whether it accepts your ID and account type. Before you earn, register as a micro-entrepreneur if your permit allows it. Label every affiliate video 'Publicité' and switch on TikTok's content-disclosure setting. Only promote products you tested; no cheap toys. At 1,000 followers, start LIVE with your voice and hands, talking to the chat in real time, for at least 30 minutes (30 minutes in 28 days is the commonly cited LIVE Subscription requirement). Never ask for gifts. Ignore agency messages that ask for any fee.

**Milestone:** 1,000 followers (LIVE unlocked); TikTok Shop affiliate status checked; first small income is possible but not guaranteed.

### Weeks 9–13 (to day 90): build toward 10K
Keep posting 3–5+ videos a week and go LIVE regularly. Research says meaningful LIVE income usually needs months of near-daily LIVEs of 1+ hour. Prepare a one-page media kit (followers, views, audience countries) and pitch small French brands in your niche; typical rates at 1K–10K followers are about €50–250 per video (https://businessbacon.fr/prix-influenceur/). Get a written contract and label every deal. Watch your 30-day views in TikTok Studio. When you reach 10,000 followers and 100,000 views in 30 days, apply to Creator Rewards in TikTok Studio > Monetization and complete your tax info and payout method. Keep a simple income record from the first euro.

**Milestone:** Be realistic. Guides claim consistent posters reach 10K in about 9–16 weeks, but those claims are biased toward success stories, and many accounts never get there. Pew found the top 25% of US adult posters make 98% of public videos (https://www.pewresearch.org/short-reads/2025/02/18/what-we-know-about-tiktok-content-creators/). A realistic goal for day 90: 1,000+ followers, a format that works, and first euros from affiliate, LIVE or a small brand deal. Creator Rewards income may start later and is often under €100–300 a month at first.

## Legal and tax (France)

1) LABEL EVERY AD. Under loi n° 2023-451 (9 June 2023), any post that promotes a product in exchange for money, a commission or a free product must say 'Publicité' or 'Collaboration commerciale'. Since ordonnance n° 2024-978 (6 Nov 2024), clear equivalent wording is also allowed. The label must be visible immediately and in French. This also covers TikTok Shop affiliate videos and gifted products (https://www.economie.gouv.fr/dgccrf/les-fiches-pratiques/promotion-faite-par-les-influenceurs-et-achat-les-reseaux-sociaux, https://uniondesmarques.fr/ressources/loi-influence-commerciale-quelles-sont-les-modifications-apportees-par-voie-d-ordonnance-en-novembre-dernier). Also switch on TikTok's own content-disclosure setting; undisclosed ads can lose visibility or be removed from For You (TikTok Community Guidelines 2026). Add 'Images retouchées' if a face or body is retouched, and 'Images virtuelles' if AI is used to show a face or body in commercial content. Penalties go up to 2 years in prison and a €300,000 fine (https://cabinetpersee.com/blog/posts/loi-influenceurs-decryptage/). The fraud authority (DGCCRF) checked 287 influencers in 2024 and found problems in nearly half (https://www.franceinfo.fr/internet/reseaux-sociaux/pres-de-la-moitie-des-287-influenceurs-controles-en-2024-par-la-repression-des-fraudes-presentaient-des-anomalies-dans-leurs-publications_7128150.html).
2) CONTRACTS. Since 1 Jan 2026 (décret n° 2025-1137), a written contract is mandatory when your deals with one partner reach €1,000 excl. VAT in a year, counting cash plus free products. Without one, the contract is void (https://www.tgs-avocats.fr/blog/influence-commerciale-de-nouvelles-obligations-contractuelles-des-janvier-2026). Get everything in writing anyway, including with any LIVE agency.
3) BANNED PROMOTIONS. Never promote: cosmetic surgery, stopping medical treatment, certain medical devices, crypto services not registered with the AMF (the French financial regulator), risky financial products, sports-betting tips or nicotine products. Gambling may only be promoted on platforms that block minors (https://cabinetpersee.com/blog/posts/loi-influenceurs-decryptage/).
4) CHILDREN. If your own children under 16 are the main subject of monetized videos, loi n° 2020-1266 applies. Depending on time and income thresholds, you need either an official authorisation or a declaration, and part of the child's earnings must be paid into a blocked Caisse des Dépôts account until age 18 (https://better-internet-for-kids.europa.eu/en/rules-guidelines/law-october-19-2020-aimed-regulating-commercial-exploitation-image-children-under).
5) AI. The EU AI Act transparency rules (Article 50) have applied since 2 Aug 2026 and were not postponed (https://www.aiactblog.nl/en/posts/article-50-transparency-deadline-2-august-2026). TikTok also requires a label on realistic AI-made scenes or people.
6) TAX. All TikTok income is taxable from the first euro: Creator Rewards, gifts, commissions, sponsorships, and free products at their market value (https://hayot-expertise.fr/blog/fiscalite-influenceur-cadeaux-revenus-plateformes-2026). Assume the tax office can see it. Small, occasional income can go on form 2042-C-PRO as 'BNC non professionnels'; social charges are cited at 17.2% but may be higher in 2026. If the income becomes regular, register as a micro-entrepreneur with URSSAF. The 2026 contribution rate on turnover is 21.2% for 'BIC services' or 25.6% for 'BNC' (https://www.shine.fr/blog/hausse-cotisations-sociales-micro-entreprise-2026/). Guides disagree on which category creator income falls under, so ask an accountant or your tax office (SIE). Below €37,500 of turnover from services you charge no VAT (https://comptabook.fr/tva/seuil-franchise-tva-2026/). Also declare this income to the CAF if you receive benefits such as RSA or the prime d'activité.
7) RESIDENCE PERMIT. As a non-EU national, you can register a micro-entreprise only if your titre de séjour allows self-employed work, for example a resident card or a 'vie privée et familiale' permit. 'Étudiant' or 'salarié' permits usually do not; check with the préfecture (https://www.indy.fr/blog/creer-micro-entreprise-etranger/).

## Warnings

- Create the account in France with your French SIM and NEVER use a VPN. The region is set at sign-up and is hard to change. VPN use disqualifies you from Creator Rewards, and an Egypt-region account cannot join it (https://tiktrendsonline.com/tiktok-creator-rewards-program-france/).
- Keep a Personal account. Business accounts are excluded from Creator Rewards (https://wise.com/fr/blog/monetisation-tiktok). If TikTok Shop asks you to switch to a professional account, check first what you would lose.
- Do NOT make a kids channel on TikTok. You must be 13+ to have an account, the under-13 version exists only in the US, videos from users under 16 cannot reach For You, and EU teens aged 13–17 get no personalised ads. A child audience is small and worth little (https://raw.githubusercontent.com/OpenTermsArchive/pga-versions/main/TikTok/Community%20Guidelines.md, https://techcrunch.com/2023/08/04/tiktok-algorithm-dsa/).
- Do not reupload your YouTube kids videos unchanged. Under TikTok's 2025 and 2026 guidelines, clips with another platform's watermark or 'reused material without anything new' are not eligible for For You, so they cannot earn.
- Never put children on a LIVE (only adults 18+ can host), and think carefully before showing your own children in monetized videos (French law 2020-1266).
- Low-effort content earns nothing: reposts, compilations, photo slideshows, a robot voice over other people's footage, and (for Creator Rewards) any video under 1 minute. Fully AI-generated videos may not qualify and usually earn close to nothing. Label realistic AI content (https://storrito.com/resources/what-tiktoks-ai-monetization-restrictions-signal-for-creator-income/).
- LIVE agency scams: never pay a joining or 'activation' fee, never give your password, and never agree to give away a share of your Diamonds. Check that any agency invitation is real inside the TikTok app (LIVE Backstage), talk to their current creators, and insist on a written contract you can leave (https://startnest.pk/blog/tiktok-live-agency-scams-vs-legit.php).
- Never buy followers or views, and never use 'unlock LIVE / unlock gifts' services. TikTok removes fake followers and likes, and you risk a ban and the loss of unpaid rewards.
- On LIVE, never ask for gifts or promise anything in exchange for gifts. Do not show QR codes or phone numbers, and do not send viewers to outside websites. Static, looped or unattended LIVEs get restricted. One report says TikTok took action against over 1 million LIVE creators in Q2 2025 (unverified figure). In TikTok Shop countries such as France, LIVEs that push buying outside TikTok also get less visibility.
- TikTok Shop: avoid cheap toys and children's products. A 2025 DGCCRF survey found about 90% of toys bought on large online marketplaces non-compliant and over 60% dangerous (not TikTok Shop specifically), and TikTok Shop France was reported as 'flooded with counterfeits' in Nov 2025. Under the influencer law, the promoter can be held liable (https://www.economie.gouv.fr/actualites/commerce-en-ligne-90-de-jouets-non-conformes-et-60-de-jouets-dangereux-sur-les-plateformes, https://www.journaldugeek.com/2025/11/28/tiktok-shop-noyee-dans-la-contrefacon/).
- Affiliate commissions can be cancelled when buyers return items, and some are reportedly held for up to 90 days (unverified).
- Do not depend on one income source. Creator Rewards pay reportedly dropped sharply for many creators in early 2026 (https://eurweb.com/tiktok-payout-drop/). LIVE gifting is under political pressure in France: a parliamentary report in Sept 2025 called gifting and matches casino-like (these were recommendations, not law), and the rules could tighten.
- Ignore courses promising '€5,000 a month'. Income is very concentrated: a secondhand CNC figure says 85% of French creators earn under the minimum wage (SMIC), and another source says 26% earn nothing (https://industries.ma/createurs-de-contenu-en-france-une-industrie-a-7-milliards-deuros-mais-85-sous-le-smic/).
- Withdraw your earnings regularly and complete payout setup (tax info and payment method). A ban can mean losing unpaid rewards, and an unclaimed Creator Rewards balance can reportedly be forfeited after 2 years.
- Avoid political content if you want to earn: government and political accounts cannot monetize, and paid political content is banned (TikTok Community Guidelines).

## Unverified — check in the app

- Whether Creator Rewards pays only for viewers in eligible countries (US, UK, Germany, France, Japan, South Korea, Brazil, Mexico). This decides whether Arabic content can earn. Check TikTok Studio > Monetization or the program terms.
- Whether views from teens aged 13–17 count, or pay less, for Creator Rewards.
